About Gram Helpline

Gram Helpline is a Digital India initiative that brings transparency and accountability to village-level governance. Designed for rural citizens, it provides a simple, accessible platform for registering local issues and tracking resolutions — all in one place.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between villagers and Gram Panchayat authorities through technology, ensuring every complaint reaches the right desk and gets resolved on time.
